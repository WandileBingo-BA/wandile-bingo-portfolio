Project Purpose

Building the complete Business Analyst lifecycle into one case study.

This project is being intentionally developed beyond a traditional dashboard exercise. The objective is to demonstrate how a Business Analyst moves from an initial business problem through stakeholder analysis, requirements, process design, solution delivery and validation.

Why this project matters

The finished case study will combine traditional Business Analysis practices with data and business intelligence capabilities, showing both the business-facing and technical sides of the role within one end-to-end project.

Planned BA Lifecycle

From business problem to implemented solution.

01 / DISCOVERY

Business problem definition

Define the business context, objectives, scope, constraints, assumptions and measurable outcomes.

02 / STAKEHOLDERS

Stakeholder analysis

Identify affected stakeholders, decision-makers, influence levels, business needs and communication requirements.

03 / REQUIREMENTS

Requirements elicitation

Translate stakeholder needs into clear business, functional and non-functional requirements.

04 / AS-IS

Current-state process analysis

Map the existing process, identify bottlenecks, control weaknesses, hand-offs, duplication and operational pain points.

05 / GAP ANALYSIS

Root cause and gap analysis

Compare current performance with the desired state and identify the underlying causes of the business problem.

06 / TO-BE

Future-state process design

Design an improved process and define how the proposed solution addresses the identified business gaps.

07 / AGILE DELIVERY

User stories and acceptance criteria

Translate requirements into Agile epics, user stories, prioritised backlog items and measurable acceptance criteria.

08 / JIRA

Workflow and backlog management

Structure and manage requirements, tasks and delivery progress within a Jira-based Agile workflow.

09 / SOLUTION

Power BI & SQL analysis

Develop the analytical solution using controlled data preparation, modelling, SQL analysis, KPI logic and management reporting.

10 / TRACEABILITY

Requirements traceability

Connect business needs to requirements, user stories, solution components and test evidence.

11 / UAT

User Acceptance Testing

Develop UAT scenarios, test cases, expected results, defect tracking and formal acceptance evidence.

12 / IMPLEMENTATION

Implementation readiness

Define implementation actions, stakeholder communication, operational risks, KPIs and post-implementation measurement.
